Complete guide

Roasted Veggie Flatbread from start to finish

This recipe is simple, colorful, and perfect for sharing. Follow these steps from prep to serving.

1. Prep the vegetables

Roast sliced peppers, zucchini, and onion with olive oil, salt, and a little oregano until lightly golden.

2. Spread the base

Lay the flatbread on a tray and smooth on a generous layer of herbed cream cheese.

3. Build the topping

Scatter the roasted vegetables over the base and finish with a few fresh herbs.

4. Bake and serve

Bake until the edges are crisp, slice into pieces, and serve warm with a light salad.
