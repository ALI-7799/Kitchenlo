Ginger Turkey Lettuce Wraps

Savoury minced turkey with ginger, garlic and water chestnuts, spooned into cold lettuce cups.

Asian-inspired dinner · Updated 16 October 2026
Prep15 min
Cook12 min
Total27 min
Serves4
DifficultyEasy
Calories326 kcal

Minced turkey is dry when it is treated like beef and excellent when it is not. The trick is a hot pan, very little stirring at the start so it browns rather than steams, and a sauce added only at the end so it glazes instead of boiling the meat. Cold crisp lettuce against the hot filling is most of the appeal.

Equipment

  • Large skillet or wok
  • Fine grater

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the sauce first

    Stir the soy sauce, vinegar, hoisin, sesame oil, honey and chilli together in a small bowl. Once the pan is hot there is no time to measure.

  2. 2

    Brown the turkey

    Heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high until it shimmers. Add the mince with the salt, spread it flat and leave it undisturbed for 4 minutes. Letting it sit is what gives you browned crust instead of grey crumbs.

  3. 3

    Break it up

    Break the meat into pieces with a wooden spoon and cook 3 to 4 minutes more, until no pink remains and some of the liquid has cooked off.

  4. 4

    Add the aromatics

    Push the meat to one side, add the garlic, ginger and spring onion whites to the clear space and stir for 45 seconds, until fragrant but not coloured, then combine everything.

  5. 5

    Glaze with the sauce

    Add the water chestnuts and pour in the sauce. Cook for 2 minutes over medium-high, stirring, until the liquid has reduced to a glaze that clings to the meat rather than pooling in the pan.

  6. 6

    Serve into cold leaves

    Stir in the spring onion greens. Spoon the hot filling into the chilled lettuce cups at the table, and finish with sesame seeds, herbs and a squeeze of lime.

Cook’s tips

  • Keep the lettuce in iced water until the moment you serve, then dry it. The contrast between cold leaf and hot filling is the point of the dish.
  • Do not stir the mince for the first few minutes. Browning is where the flavour comes from, and turkey gives you very little of it otherwise.
  • Reduce the sauce until it glazes. A wet filling will collapse the lettuce and run down your wrist.

Variations

  • Use chicken, pork or plant mince, or crumbled firm tofu pressed dry for a vegan version with tamari in place of the hoisin.
  • Serve the filling over rice or rice noodles instead of lettuce for a more substantial dinner.
  • Add a diced red pepper or a cup of shredded cabbage with the water chestnuts for more vegetables.

Storage and make-ahead

The filling keeps 3 days in the fridge and freezes for 2 months. Reheat it in a hot pan rather than the microwave so the glaze tightens again, and prepare the lettuce fresh each time.

Ginger Turkey Lettuce Wraps: your questions answered

Can I make this gluten-free?

Yes. Use tamari in place of the soy sauce and check the hoisin, as most brands contain wheat; a gluten-free hoisin or an extra teaspoon of honey with a splash more tamari both work.

Which lettuce holds up best?

Baby gem is the sturdiest and easiest to eat. Iceberg gives bigger cups and more crunch but tears, and butter lettuce is the most tender but needs a double leaf.

My turkey came out dry. Why?

Turkey breast mince is very lean and overcooks in about a minute. Buy thigh mince if your shop has it, pull the pan off as soon as the glaze thickens, and do not hold it hot on the stove while you prepare the lettuce.
