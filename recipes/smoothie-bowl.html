Green Protein Smoothie Bowl

A thick, spoonable blend of spinach, banana, Greek yogurt and protein, built to keep you full until lunch.

International breakfast · Updated 12 July 2026
Prep8 min
CookNone
Total8 min
Serves2
DifficultyEasy
Calories412 kcal

The difference between a smoothie and a smoothie bowl is water, and most recipes use too much of it. Frozen banana plus thick yogurt and only a splash of milk gives you something you eat with a spoon. The spinach disappears entirely behind the fruit.

Equipment

  • High-speed blender
  • Tamper or spatula

Method

  1. 1

    Load the blender in order

    Put the milk, yogurt, spinach and almond butter in first, then the frozen banana and protein powder on top. Liquid at the bottom lets the blades catch.

  2. 2

    Blend low, then high

    Start on low to break everything down, then move to high for 45-60 seconds. Use the tamper to push the mixture into the blades rather than adding more liquid.

  3. 3

    Check the texture

    It should be thick enough to hold the shape of a spoon dragged through it. Add milk only one tablespoon at a time if the blender genuinely stalls.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust

    Blend in the vanilla and taste. Add a date or a little honey if your bananas were underripe.

  5. 5

    Bowl it

    Divide between two chilled bowls and smooth the surface with the back of a spoon.

  6. 6

    Top generously

    Arrange the berries, chia, granola and coconut in sections, then drizzle with honey. Eat straight away while it is still cold and thick.

Cook’s tips

  • Freeze the bananas. Fresh banana plus ice makes a watery, icy bowl; frozen banana makes a creamy one.
  • Chill the bowls in the freezer for ten minutes and the smoothie holds its texture twice as long.
  • Blend the spinach thoroughly before adding the frozen fruit so you never get green flecks.

Variations

  • Swap spinach for frozen cauliflower rice for an even thicker bowl with no fruit sugar.
  • Use frozen mango and pineapple for a tropical version that hides the greens just as well.
  • Make it vegan with coconut yogurt and a plant protein; add an extra tablespoon of nut butter for creaminess.

Storage and make-ahead

Meant to be eaten immediately. You can freeze the base in ice-cube trays for up to a month, then re-blend with a splash of milk.

Green Protein Smoothie Bowl: your questions answered

Can I make it without protein powder?

Yes. Add an extra half cup of Greek yogurt plus two tablespoons of hemp seeds and you will land around 22 g of protein.

Why is my bowl runny?

Too much liquid, or fruit that was not frozen. Start with less milk than you think you need; you can always add, but you cannot take it out.

Can I prep this the night before?

Portion the frozen banana, spinach and protein powder into a freezer bag. In the morning you just tip the bag in with the yogurt and milk.
