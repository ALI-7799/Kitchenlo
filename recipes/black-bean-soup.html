Smoky Black Bean Soup

A thick, smoky black bean soup from tins and store-cupboard spices, blended half smooth in 40 minutes.

Latin-inspired dinner · Updated 16 October 2026
Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Total40 min
Serves6
DifficultyEasy
Calories312 kcal

Blending only half the pot is the detail that makes this worth making. You get the body of a pureed soup and still have whole beans to chew on, without any cream or stock cubes doing the work. Smoked paprika and a little cumin give it the depth that usually comes from a ham bone and several hours.

How to make Smoky Black Bean Soup

Equipment

  • Large heavy pot
  • Stick blender or jug blender
  • Wooden spoon

Method

  1. 1

    Soften the vegetables

    Heat the oil in a large pot over medium and cook the onion, celery and pepper with a pinch of salt for 8 minutes, until soft and just starting to colour at the edges. This is the base of the flavour, so do not rush it.

  2. 2

    Toast the spices

    Add the garlic, cumin, paprika, oregano and chipotle and stir for 60 seconds, until the pot smells strongly of spice. Toasting them in oil is what separates a deep soup from a dusty one.

  3. 3

    Build the pot

    Add the beans, tomatoes, stock, bay leaf and salt. Scrape the bottom of the pot to lift anything stuck, bring it to a boil, then lower to a steady simmer.

  4. 4

    Simmer

    Simmer uncovered for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until the liquid has reduced slightly and the beans are completely soft.

  5. 5

    Blend half

    Remove the bay leaf. Blend about half the pot with a stick blender, or take 3 cups out to a blender and return it. Stop while there are still plenty of whole beans visible.

  6. 6

    Finish with lime

    Stir in the lime juice and taste for salt; it will need more than you expect. Serve with coriander, chilli, avocado and more lime at the table.

Cook’s tips

  • Rinse the beans properly. The liquid in the tin makes the soup taste tinny and dulls the smoked paprika.
  • The lime at the end is not a garnish. Without acid the soup tastes heavy and flat, however well seasoned it is.
  • If it thickens too much on standing, loosen it with stock or water rather than more tomato, which would make it sharp.

Variations

  • Add 200 g of chorizo or smoked bacon with the onion if you are not keeping it vegan.
  • Stir in a cup of sweetcorn or a chopped courgette in the last 5 minutes for more vegetables.
  • Serve it thicker over rice as a stew, or thinner with tortilla chips crushed on top.

Storage and make-ahead

Keeps 5 days in the fridge, thickening as it sits, and freezes well for 3 months. Freeze it before adding the lime and the toppings, and reheat gently with a splash of water.

Smoky Black Bean Soup: your questions answered

Can I use dried black beans?

Yes. 400 g of dried beans soaked overnight and simmered until tender replaces the three tins, and the bean cooking liquid can stand in for some of the stock. Allow an extra hour.

Is it meant to be spicy?

Only mildly as written. The chipotle is there for smoke rather than heat, so adjust it freely, and put the sliced chilli on the table so everyone can choose.

What makes it taste smoky without meat?

Smoked paprika and chipotle, both toasted in oil before the liquid goes in. A teaspoon of liquid smoke or a roasted red pepper blended in pushes it further if you want more.
