Chocolate Banana Pops

Frozen banana halves dipped in dark chocolate and rolled in nuts, sprinkles or coconut.

American dessert · Updated 15 June 2026
Prep15 min
CookNone
Total15 min
Serves8
DifficultyEasy
Calories198 kcal

Three ingredients and a freezer. The trick is freezing the bananas solid before dipping, so the warm chocolate sets into a hard shell on contact rather than sliding off. Add a spoon of coconut oil to the chocolate and it snaps when you bite it.

8

Pops

Coatings

Equipment

  • Baking tray
  • Baking parchment
  • 8 wooden sticks
  • Heatproof bowl

Method

  1. 1

    Skewer and freeze

    Peel the bananas, halve them crossways and push a stick into the cut end of each. Lay on a parchment-lined tray and freeze at least 2 hours, until rock solid.

  2. 2

    Prepare the coatings

    Put each coating in its own shallow bowl and line a second tray with parchment. Once dipping starts there is no time to prepare anything.

  3. 3

    Melt the chocolate

    Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil in short 20-second bursts in the microwave, stirring between each, or over a pan of barely simmering water. Let it cool for 2 minutes.

  4. 4

    Dip fast

    Working with one frozen banana at a time, dip and turn to coat, letting the excess drip off. The chocolate will begin setting within seconds against the frozen fruit.

  5. 5

    Coat immediately

    Roll or sprinkle the coating on straight away, before the shell hardens, then set on the lined tray.

  6. 6

    Firm up

    Return to the freezer for 15 minutes to set completely. Serve straight from frozen, or let them soften for 3 minutes for a softer bite.

Cook’s tips

  • Freeze the bananas solid first. Room-temperature bananas make the chocolate slide off and go grey.
  • The coconut oil is what makes the shell snap; without it the chocolate coating is dull and soft.
  • Take one banana out of the freezer at a time. Chocolate sets on contact, so you have only a few seconds to add toppings.

Variations

  • Use white or milk chocolate, or drizzle a second colour over the set shell.
  • Swap banana for frozen strawberries or grapes threaded onto skewers.
  • Spread peanut butter along the banana before freezing for a hidden layer.

Storage and make-ahead

Keeps 1 month in the freezer. Once fully set, wrap each pop individually or layer between parchment in an airtight box so they do not stick together.

Chocolate Banana Pops: your questions answered

Why did my chocolate seize?

Water got into it, usually steam from a double boiler or a wet bowl. Everything must be bone dry, and melt in short bursts rather than continuous heat.

What bananas should I use?

Ripe enough to be sweet but still firm. Overripe bananas go mushy in the freezer and fall off the stick during dipping.

Can I make them dairy free?

They already are if you use dark chocolate; just check the label, as some dark chocolate contains milk solids.
