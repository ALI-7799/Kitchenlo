Honey Soy Chicken Skillet

Tender chicken and crisp vegetables glazed in a sweet-savoury honey soy sauce that reduces to a lacquer.

Asian-inspired dinner · Updated 20 August 2026
Prep10 min
Cook16 min
Total26 min
Serves4
DifficultyEasy
Calories468 kcal

Four pantry ingredients make the glaze, and the pan does the rest. The sauce reduces around the chicken until it turns sticky and dark, catching on the broccoli and peppers. Serve it over rice and there is nothing left in the pan worth washing off.

4

Chicken and vegetables

Honey soy glaze

To serve

Equipment

  • Large skillet or wok
  • Small bowl

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the glaze

    Stir the soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar and sesame oil together in a small bowl. Keep the cornflour slurry separate for now.

  2. 2

    Brown the chicken

    Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the chicken in a single layer and cook 5-6 minutes, turning once, until deeply golden. Move it to a plate.

  3. 3

    Cook the vegetables

    Add the broccoli and pepper to the pan with a splash of water. Cover and steam 3 minutes, then uncover and toss until the broccoli is bright green and just tender.

  4. 4

    Bloom the aromatics

    Push the vegetables aside, add the garlic and ginger to the bare pan and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.

  5. 5

    Glaze

    Return the chicken, pour in the sauce and bring to a simmer. Stir in the cornflour slurry and cook 1-2 minutes until the glaze thickens and coats everything in a shiny layer.

  6. 6

    Serve

    Spoon over steamed rice and finish with sesame seeds and spring onion.

Cook’s tips

  • Add the cornflour slurry only once the sauce is simmering, or it will not thicken properly.
  • Thighs stay juicy under a hot glaze in a way breast meat does not; if you use breast, cut the browning time to 4 minutes.
  • Use low-sodium soy sauce. Full-strength soy plus reduction can push this past salty.

Variations

  • Add a tablespoon of gochujang or sriracha to the glaze for a sweet-hot version.
  • Swap in green beans, snap peas or bok choy depending on what is in the drawer.
  • Make it with firm tofu or salmon fillets, glazing them in the last two minutes.

Storage and make-ahead

Keeps 3 days refrigerated and reheats well, as the glaze loosens with a splash of water. It also freezes for up to 2 months without the rice.

Honey Soy Chicken Skillet: your questions answered

Why is my glaze thin?

Either the slurry went in before the sauce was simmering, or it needed another minute. Keep it bubbling and stir constantly; it thickens quite suddenly.

Can I make this ahead for lunches?

Yes. Cook it fully, cool quickly, and portion over rice. The flavour actually deepens by the next day.

What can I use instead of honey?

Maple syrup works one-for-one and keeps it vegan-friendly. Brown sugar also works, using three tablespoons dissolved into the soy sauce.
