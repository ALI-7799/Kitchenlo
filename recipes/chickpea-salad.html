Mediterranean Chickpea Salad

Chickpeas, crisp cucumber, olives and feta in a lemon-oregano dressing that gets better as it sits.

Mediterranean lunch · Updated 5 August 2026
Prep15 min
CookNone
Total15 min
Serves4
DifficultyEasy
Calories384 kcal

This is the salad that survives a packed lunch. Nothing in it wilts, the dressing soaks into the chickpeas over a few hours, and it tastes better on day two than day one. Make a big bowl on Sunday and eat from it all week.

Ingredients

4

Quantities scale automatically. Cooking times stay the same.

Salad

Lemon oregano dressing

Equipment

  • Large mixing bowl
  • Jar for the dressing

Method

  1. 1

    Dry the chickpeas

    Drain, rinse and then roll the chickpeas in a clean tea towel. Dry chickpeas absorb dressing instead of diluting it.

  2. 2

    Seed the cucumber

    Halve the cucumber lengthways and scrape out the watery seed core with a teaspoon before dicing. This is what stops the salad going soupy by day three.

  3. 3

    Shake the dressing

    Put the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, honey, salt and pepper in a jar and shake hard for 20 seconds until thickened and emulsified.

  4. 4

    Soak the onion

    Cover the diced red onion with cold water for 5 minutes, then drain. It takes the harsh bite out without losing the crunch.

  5. 5

    Combine

    Toss the chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, drained onion, olives and herbs with three-quarters of the dressing.

  6. 6

    Finish with feta

    Fold the feta through last so the cubes stay intact. Rest 20 minutes before eating, then add the remaining dressing to taste.

Cook’s tips

  • Dress it at least twenty minutes ahead. Chickpeas are bland on their own and need time to take on the lemon and garlic.
  • Save the chickpea liquid (aquafaba) for baking or for whipping into a vegan mousse rather than pouring it away.
  • If packing lunches, keep the feta and dressing in a separate pot and combine in the morning.

Variations

  • Add a tin of tuna or shredded chicken for a higher-protein version.
  • Fold in cooked orzo or couscous to stretch it into a grain salad.
  • Skip the feta and add extra olives and a spoon of tahini for a vegan bowl.

Storage and make-ahead

Keeps 4 days in the fridge and genuinely improves after the first day. Add the fresh herbs on the day of eating if you want them bright green.

Mediterranean Chickpea Salad: your questions answered

Can I use dried chickpeas?

Yes, and the texture is better. Soak 1 cup overnight, simmer 45-60 minutes until tender, and cool fully before dressing. One cup dried gives roughly the same as two tins.

How do I stop it going watery?

Seed the cucumber, dry the chickpeas properly, and salt the tomatoes separately for 10 minutes then drain them before adding.

Is this filling enough as a main?

For most people, yes, at 384 calories and 10 g of fibre per portion. If you want it more substantial, add half an avocado or a scoop of grains per bowl.
