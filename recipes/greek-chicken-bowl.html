Greek Chicken Rice Bowl

Yogurt-marinated chicken thighs over brown rice with a chopped salad, feta and quick tzatziki.

Greek dinner · Updated 16 October 2026
Prep20 min
Cook15 min
Total35 min
Serves4
DifficultyEasy
Calories572 kcal

The marinade does the work here. Yogurt holds the lemon and oregano against the meat and its acidity tenderises the thighs, so even fifteen minutes makes a visible difference and a few hours makes a great one. Everything else is assembly, and every component keeps separately for lunches later in the week.

How to make Greek Chicken Rice Bowl

Ingredients

4

Quantities scale automatically. Cooking times stay the same.

Marinated chicken

Bowls

Quick tzatziki

Equipment

  • Heavy skillet or griddle pan
  • Box grater
  • Saucepan for the rice

Method

  1. 1

    Marinate the chicken

    Mix the yogurt, olive oil, lemon juice and zest, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper in a bowl, add the thighs and turn them until coated. Leave 15 minutes on the counter, or up to 8 hours in the fridge.

  2. 2

    Cook the rice

    Start the brown rice in plenty of salted water, about 25 minutes, so it is ready when the chicken is. Drain it and leave it covered for 5 minutes to steam.

  3. 3

    Make the tzatziki

    Squeeze the grated cucumber hard in a clean tea towel; this is what keeps the sauce thick. Stir it through the yogurt with the garlic, lemon juice, oil and a good pinch of salt.

  4. 4

    Sear the chicken

    Heat a dry heavy pan over medium-high. Scrape most of the marinade off the thighs and lay them in with space between. Cook 6 to 7 minutes a side, until charred in patches and cooked to 75C / 165F at the thickest point.

  5. 5

    Rest and slice

    Move the chicken to a board and rest it 5 minutes before slicing it thickly across the grain. Slicing straight away loses the juices to the board.

  6. 6

    Build the bowls

    Divide the rice between four bowls, add the tomatoes, cucumber, onion and olives in sections, then the sliced chicken. Add a spoon of tzatziki, scatter the feta and herbs, and finish with olive oil and a squeeze of lemon.

Cook’s tips

  • Scrape the thick marinade off before the chicken hits the pan. Left on, the yogurt burns before the meat is cooked through.
  • Thighs are more forgiving than breasts here and stay juicy even if they go a minute long. If you only have breasts, pound them to an even thickness and cut the time to 4 minutes a side.
  • Salt the tomatoes and cucumber 10 minutes ahead and the juices become a dressing on their own.

Variations

  • Swap the rice for orzo, quinoa or a bed of shredded romaine for a lower-carb bowl.
  • Use the same marinade on halloumi or firm tofu for a vegetarian version.
  • Add a scoop of hummus and warm pitta and serve it as a mezze plate instead.

Storage and make-ahead

Store the components separately in the fridge for up to 4 days; the chicken and rice reheat well, and the salad and tzatziki stay fresh because they were never heated. Cooked chicken freezes for 2 months.

Greek Chicken Rice Bowl: your questions answered

How long should I marinate the chicken?

Fifteen minutes gives you flavour on the surface, and 4 to 8 hours gives you noticeably more tender meat. Past about 12 hours the yogurt starts to make the texture soft rather than tender.

Can I cook it on a barbecue?

Yes, and it is better for it. Grill over direct medium heat for 5 to 6 minutes a side, with the lid down between turns.

Why is my tzatziki watery?

The cucumber was not squeezed dry. It holds a surprising amount of water, and it leaks into the yogurt within the hour if you skip that step.
